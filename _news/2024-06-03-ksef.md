My research paper, *"The Method Improving LLM Language Understanding through the Application of Critical Thinking,"* was accepted and presented at the 22nd Korea Science and Engineering Fair (KSEF). The paper explores mechanisms to suppress hallucination in LLMs using prompt-based cognitive structures.
