Officially began independent research on **J.A.R.V.I.S.**, an architecture for autonomous multi-agent orchestration. By structuring agents with strict personas (e.g., Developer, QA Auditor) and integrating Docker sandbox evaluation, the system aims to mitigate LLM hallucinations in complex coding tasks.
