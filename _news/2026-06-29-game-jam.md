Participated in the 2026 Department Game Jam, where our team developed a 2D adventure game. We utilized **Harness Engineering** to rapidly prototype the game without writing extensive code. This experience taught me the immense benefits of fast prototyping, but also the critical importance of strict code inspection and architecture when combining no-code tools.
