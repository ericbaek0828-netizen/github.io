## 💡 The Philosophy of Teaching

Inspired by Dr. Andrew Huberman's approach to neuroplasticity and learning, I firmly believe that the ultimate test of understanding is the ability to teach. Throughout my academic journey, I have actively sought out roles as a **Teaching Assistant (TA)** and mentor, treating knowledge sharing not just as a duty, but as a core method for solidifying my own expertise.

---

## 🏫 CodingLab: Lead Instructor & Founder (2024)

During my high school years at Incheon Youngsun High School, I re-founded and led **CodingLab (코딩랩)**, a student programming club. My goal was to demystify complex artificial intelligence concepts for my peers.

As the lead instructor, I designed a comprehensive, semester-long curriculum covering the mathematical and structural foundations of neural networks. I prepared over 80 pages of original lecture slides and delivered weekly sessions.

### Curriculum Highlights:
- **Perceptrons:** Taught the basics of synaptic weights, biases, and activation functions (Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU). Covered loss functions (BCE, MSE) and the intuition behind Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD).
- **CNN (Convolutional Neural Networks):** Explained convolution operations, kernel/filter sliding, pooling, and how dimensional reduction extracts spatial features.
- **RNN (Recurrent Neural Networks):** Guided students through sequence data handling, hidden states, vanishing/exploding gradients, and introduced advanced architectures like LSTM, GRU, and basic Attention mechanisms.

### Impact:
This experience cultivated my ability to break down highly technical AI research into digestible concepts, directly building the communication and leadership skills I now utilize as an Undergraduate Researcher and Teaching Assistant.
